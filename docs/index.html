Everything’s in your hotbar

Nine things that make it cosy. Pick a slot, or press 1 to 9 like you would in game.

Play any version

Make as many instances as you like. Each one keeps its own version, mods, worlds and settings, with Vanilla or Fabric for every version.

  • Java downloads itself, so there's nothing to set up
  • Sign in with Microsoft; your login stays on your own PC
  • Join a server automatically when you press Play

Mods in one click

Search Modrinth from inside the launcher. You only see mods made for your instance's version, and anything a mod needs comes along too.

  • Whole Modrinth modpacks in one click
  • Change an instance's version and its mods update to match
  • Mods are checked every time you press Play: wrong versions get updated, and ones that can't work yet are switched off instead of crashing
  • Turn mods on or off, or remove them

Make it yours

Twelve themes, or any colour you like. Add a background, pick a font, and put the menu wherever feels right.

  • Custom colour picker for background and accent
  • Built-in catgirl art, your own picture or any image link, with dim and blur sliders
  • Cute fonts, or any font on your PC
  • Menu on the left, right, top or bottom, with or without labels

Up and running in a minute

Three advancements to your first launch. No setup, no Java, no fuss.

  1. Grab the installer

    Download it with the button above and open it. If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway.

  2. Sign in

    Add your Microsoft account. The launcher shows a short code; type it on Microsoft's page and you're in.

  3. Make an instance and play

    Pick a Minecraft version, choose Fabric if you want mods, and press Play. The first launch takes a few minutes.

You'll need Windows 10 or 11 and a Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition. Updates install themselves.
